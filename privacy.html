Privacy Policy & Data Protection Disclosure

At TraitCompass, your privacy is protected by architectural design. We operate under a strict Zero-Health-Data Storage principle.

Key Architectural Principle: 100% Client-Side Execution

All interactive calculations—including your Color Vision Test plate responses, response durations, scoring metrics, and psychological quiz selections—are processed strictly in your local device memory using client-side JavaScript. Your health test results never travel over the network to any database or server. When you close or refresh your browser tab, test data is completely cleared from memory.

1. Information We Collect

Because we prioritize maximum privacy for our visitors, our platform does not require user accounts, emails, or personal identifiers:

  • Local Preferences (HTML5 LocalStorage): We store two simple local flags in your browser: tc_theme (for dark/light mode) and tc_contrast (for high-contrast accessibility). No personal identity is associated with these flags.
  • Standard Server Logs: Like all standard web hosting providers, server log files automatically log non-identifiable technical data (such as browser user-agent, IP address, referral URL, and timestamps) solely for DDoS mitigation, caching, and server health.

2. Advertising & Monetization Disclosures

To maintain free access to our screening tools and scientific health guides without charging user subscription fees, TraitCompass displays advertisements managed via standard programmatic publisher networks (e.g. Google AdSense / Google Marketing Platform).

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the AboutAds Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out portal.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device. We do not use first-party tracking cookies. Third-party ad networks or analytics services may set standard operational cookies. You can configure your browser to reject all cookies or alert you when a cookie is sent.

4. Compliance with Regional Privacy Frameworks

GDPR (European Union): We process all test screening features without capturing personally identifiable health data (Special Category Data under Article 9 GDPR).

CCPA / CPRA (California, USA): We do not sell or share personal health information. You have the right to visit the platform without discrimination.

COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act): Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit personal information from minors.
