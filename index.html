Interactive Ishihara Color Vision Test

Screen your spectral color perception across 20 calibrated pseudo-isochromatic plates. Receive instant screening results and educational insights on color perception variations.

ℹ️
Important Screening Disclaimer: This online color-vision screening tool is intended for educational and informational purposes only. It is not a medical examination and cannot diagnose a color-vision deficiency or other eye condition. Results may be affected by your display, brightness, lighting, and other factors. If you have concerns about your color vision, consult a qualified eye-care professional.

Pre-Test Calibration & Setup Guidelines

Because digital displays reproduce color differently than printed Ishihara plates, please adjust your device settings for optimal screening consistency:

☀️

Screen Brightness

Set your display brightness to 70% or higher. Avoid direct overhead glare or reflections.

👓

Disable Color Filters

Turn off Night Shift, TrueTone, f.lux, or blue-light blocking tinted lenses.

📏

Viewing Distance

Sit at an arm’s length (~50–75 cm) directly facing your screen without tilting.

⏱️

Pacing & Ambience

Spend 3–5 seconds per plate in a moderately lit room with neutral ambient light.

Estimated duration: ~3 minutes • 100% Free & Private (Zero Data Stored or Transmitted)
